English for the Arts

Practice English with the topics of your degree: Digital Art, Visual Arts and Cinematography. Meet a film crew, describe your studio, design game characters, plan an exhibition, pitch a film and more. Choose a unit, follow the five steps, and check what you can do at the end. Every unit takes about 50–60 minutes.

How every unit works

  1. PresentationMeet the topic in a real situation.
  2. ObjectivesSee what you will be able to do.
  3. ExplanationVocabulary, grammar and examples.
  4. PracticeFour skills:ListeningReadingWritingSpeaking
  5. Self-assessmentRate six "I can…" statements from 1 to 5.
Writing evidence: write and submit your text directly in the unit.
Speaking evidence: record your task and upload it to your course platform.
